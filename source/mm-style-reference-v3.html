Systems before people. Always.

The problem is never Susan. The problem is the system Susan has been quietly compensating for. No finding in our report will ever name a person as the constraint.

No software recommendations in week one.

New tools laid on top of unclear structure produce faster confusion. We won’t propose a platform until we can describe the process it’s meant to run.

Operators get interviewed, not just leaders.

The people executing the process every day know where it breaks. They are rarely asked. That asymmetry is where most of the findings live.

You own the findings.

The report is yours whether or not you ever work with us again. It isn’t written to require us, and it isn’t withheld to create leverage.

We’ll tell you what isn’t broken.

Consultants are structurally incentivized to find problems everywhere. Sometimes three of your five systems are healthy, and saying so is part of the job.

Every workaround has interest.

Nothing gets removed before we understand what it was paying for. Delete the workaround, keep the debt, and you’ve made the situation worse with excellent intentions.

The part clients remember

PBJ Sessions™

Structured cross-functional working sessions where marketing, sales, and operations map the same process at the same time — out loud, in front of each other.

It sounds simple. It is reliably the most uncomfortable and most valuable ninety minutes of the engagement.

These sessions are where Shadow Systems™ surface and the room gets very productive.

Who’s in the room

  • The founder or CEO
  • Whoever owns marketing
  • Whoever owns sales
  • Whoever owns operations or delivery
  • At least one person who does the work daily

The rules

  • We map what actually happens, not what should
  • Workarounds get documented, not corrected
  • No one is defending a number
  • Systems before people. Always.
