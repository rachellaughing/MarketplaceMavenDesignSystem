# Marketplace Maven Visual Prompting System

Source: "Brand Prompting & Guardrails" (Marketplace Maven brand docs). This defines the visual/illustration guardrails for AI-generated imagery — separate from UI tokens (see `tokens/`). Three categories, together forming Marketplace Maven's visual operating system.

**Golden Rule** — every visual should prioritize, in order: operational clarity, systems relationships, information hierarchy, emotional recognition — *before* aesthetics. The goal is not to make business look exciting. The goal is to make invisible operational systems visible.

## 1. Maven Utility Icons

Human-centered operational iconography for workflows, systems, and infrastructure.

- **Use for:** workflows, CRM systems, automation visuals, onboarding diagrams, SOP visuals, dashboards, process explanations, intake flows, communication systems, implementation guides.
- **Visual characteristics:** thick rounded linework, slightly imperfect geometry, hand-drawn feel, simplified operational objects, rounded corners, minimal shading, restrained color blocking, clean negative space, process arrows, system loops, consistent stroke weight, flat matte rendering.
- **Should feel like:** operational, approachable, structured, process-oriented, infrastructure-focused, calm, intelligent, dependable, organized, thoughtful.
- **Never:** futuristic UI, glossy SaaS icons, cartoon mascots, neon tech aesthetics, 3D rendering, robotic visuals, crypto aesthetics, meme styling, cluttered iconography, exaggerated gradients, glassmorphism, overly cute illustrations, chaotic layouts.

## 2. Maven Comic

Editorial systems storytelling that visualizes operational friction, founder dependency, and revenue architecture through grounded metaphor.

- **Use for:** thought leadership visuals, diagnostic storytelling, founder pain points, operational metaphors, CRM chaos examples, before/after system illustrations, blog visuals, "this is what's actually happening" storytelling.
- **Visual characteristics:** watercolor washes, paper grain textures, inked outlines, soft muted rendering, environmental storytelling, process diagrams integrated into scenes, hand annotations, blueprint influences, realistic operational environments, layered compositions, cross-section visual storytelling, muted architectural palette, embedded process arrows.
- **Should feel like:** thoughtful, diagnostic, emotionally grounded, calm, observant, operationally mature, analytical, relatable, intelligent, "slightly overwhelmed but solvable," editorial, human-centered, quietly frustrated, structured beneath the chaos.
- **Never:** superhero comic styling, exaggerated expressions, startup hustle visuals, giant upward growth arrows, motivational poster energy, glossy rendering, meme humor, cartoon mascots, loud CTA visuals, hyper-saturated colors, "girlboss" aesthetics, salesy compositions, aggressive marketing energy, overly polished corporate art.

## 3. Maven Blueprint

Structured ecosystem mapping for how operational systems, offers, and governance layers connect.

- **Use for:** ecosystem maps, offer architecture diagrams, revenue system visuals, capability maps, governance frameworks, operating models, consulting deliverables, transformation maps, maturity frameworks, organizational relationship diagrams.
- **Visual characteristics:** modular architecture panels, ecosystem mapping, directional arrows, layered hierarchy, containment boxes, consulting-grade spacing, structured typography, restrained color palette, editorial infographic styling, blueprint-inspired layouts, clean visual hierarchy, subtle texture overlays, flat matte rendering.
- **Should feel like:** strategic, calm, operationally mature, structured, intelligent, diagnostic, thoughtful, organized, "authoritative without hype," systems-oriented, editorial, consulting-grade, measurable, infrastructure-focused, intentionally designed.
- **Never:** loud sales graphics, viral infographic styling, startup hype visuals, flashy SaaS presentation aesthetics, giant growth arrows, overdecorated layouts, cluttered information hierarchy, glossy rendering, neon gradients, "10x growth" energy, meme-style graphics, influencer-style branding, excessive visual effects, attention-hacking design.
