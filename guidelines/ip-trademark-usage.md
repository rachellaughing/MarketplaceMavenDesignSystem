# Marketplace Maven — IP Language & Trademark Usage Guide

Source: "IP stack rules /draft" (Marketplace Maven brand docs). Governs how proprietary terms are styled, capitalized, and used — relevant to the design system as content/copy rules, not visual tokens. Not legal advice; for review by an IP attorney.

## Master brand

**Marketplace Maven™** — the parent consulting and systems strategy brand. Use as company name, consulting brand, master framework owner, publishing entity. Avoid: shortening to "MM" in customer-facing materials, inconsistent capitalization, using generic abbreviations before brand authority exists.

## Category / philosophy language

**Revenue Architecture** — a *category descriptor*, not a standalone trademarked brand identity. Use descriptively in website copy, thought leadership, proposals. Do NOT: present it as the company name, use ® without legal approval, imply exclusive ownership of the phrase, position it as a standalone consulting entity. Preferred framing: "Marketplace Maven's Revenue Architecture Matrix™" rather than "The Revenue Architecture Matrix™" in isolation.

## Proprietary frameworks

**Revenue Architecture Matrix™** (also referenced as the Revenue Health Matrix™ elsewhere in the business) — the core proprietary diagnostic/scoring methodology. Optional internal abbreviation RAM™, used carefully and primarily internally or in software/dashboard environments.

**Maven Revenue Diagnostic™** — the flagship assessment product / consulting entry point.

## Supporting concepts (always capitalize, use ™ until legal counsel advises otherwise)

- **Shadow Systems™** — undocumented, informal, compensatory workflows created when official systems fail. Do not dilute by using casually for every inefficiency.
- **PBJ Sessions™** — structured cross-functional working sessions that surface operational reality and Shadow Systems™ across departments. Don't over-explain the acronym in marketing materials — curiosity increases memorability.
- **Revenue Friction™** — operational resistance/inefficiencies that slow or distort revenue flow.
- **Visibility Debt™** — the accumulated operational cost of poor systems visibility. Use primarily in executive-level thought leadership and scaling conversations.

## Usage rules

- Use ™ consistently for all the terms above until legal counsel advises otherwise. Do NOT use ® unless officially federally registered.
- Always capitalize: Revenue Architecture, Revenue Architecture Matrix™, Shadow Systems™, PBJ Sessions™, Revenue Friction™, Visibility Debt™ — treat as branded concepts, not generic phrases.
- Pair "Revenue Architecture" frequently with Marketplace Maven branding to reinforce category ownership, rather than using it in isolation.

## Future expansion areas (not yet live marks)

Certification programs, software scoring systems, maturity indices, implementation playbooks, partner licensing. Potential future marks noted in source doc: Revenue Visibility Score™, Revenue Flow Map™, Revenue Systems Alignment™, Certified Revenue Architect™, MavenOS™.
